Product

A brain for your document archive

A fully local private document intelligence system that turns thousands of loose files into a knowledge base you can question.

Your data stays put

Runs 100% on your own hardware. No cloud, no external APIs — nobody reads your files. Not even us.

Every format

PDF (scanned or text), DOC/DOCX, RTF, TXT, PPT/PPTX, Excel, ZIP/RAR archives and images (JPG, PNG, TIFF…) — 10,000+ documents. New and changed files are picked up and indexed automatically.

Truly understands Vietnamese

OCR tuned for Vietnamese text: diacritics, company stamps and handwritten signatures are detected and recorded (“signed” status). Complex tables are reconstructed cell by cell and re-read with OCR — even tables that break commercial engines.

Cited answers

Every answer points to the exact file and location — auditable and verifiable; nobody has to take your word for it. And if the answer isn't in your documents, KINESIS says exactly that — it never invents one.

Self-classifying archive

Each document is classified (contract, invoice, report…) with value, dates, parties and signature status — filter, don't just search. Duplicate files are detected automatically: if you delete the original, the next run restores it from its copy; data for deleted files can be purged from the database on request.

Ask in your language

Query in Vietnamese, English or Russian — retrieval stays native to the document's language, no translation step.

Process

Four steps, one answer

From a chaotic folder of files to a precise, cited answer — all running locally.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Scans your folders and reads every format; OCR handles blurry, skewed scans, corrupted files and complex tables.

  2. 02

    Organize

    Each file is classified and stripped of metadata — value, dates, parties — summarized bilingually (VN/EN) and stored with structure.

  3. 03

    Ask

    KINESIS understands what you're actually asking about — which documents, which period, which conditions — and searches all of them at once.

  4. 04

    Answer

    One precise answer with file citations — in seconds.

What you need to start

Minimum hardware

One workstation in your office — RTX 3090 with 64GB RAM is the minimum we recommend. Bigger archives or a faster first read = more GPUs.

First run

About 1,000 documents (scans included) are fully read overnight on standard hardware. Larger archives take proportionally longer — you get the estimate before we start.

Live in 1–2 days

Setup, loading and tuning — counted from the day your files are handed over.
